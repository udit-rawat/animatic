## Style Prompt
Memory Wall is a clinical product-demo explainer, not a cyberpunk AI trailer. The visual language should feel like a premium systems tool from a company such as Apple, Stripe, or Linear: bright canvas, precise hierarchy, restrained motion, soft physical depth, and object-based explanation. Scenes should read as one continuous spatial world where cards, graph edges, status chips, and terminal surfaces transform into one another. The viewer should feel clarity, confidence, and structure.

## Colors
- `#F7F7F4` — canvas background
- `#FFFFFF` — card and surface background
- `#111111` — primary text
- `#5F6B76` — secondary text
- `#E7EBF0` — borders and dividers
- `#2563FF` — structural accent and graph links
- `#E5484D` — error and severity accent
- `#16A34A` — confirmation and success accent
- `#F3EEE7` — warm depth tint

## Typography
- `SF Pro Display`, `SF Pro Text`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, sans-serif
- `SF Mono`, `ui-monospace`, `Menlo`, monospace

## What NOT to Do
- No neon cyan or purple glows
- No black cyberpunk background
- No glitch transitions, blink cuts, or flashy wipes
- No giant gradient text or decorative effect that does not explain the system
- No dense caption slabs that overpower the animated objects
